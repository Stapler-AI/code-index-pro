## Headline endpoints — both, stated plainly

- **Pooled ΣA2/ΣA0 = 1.158** (ΣA2 = 3,753,473 vs ΣA0 = 3,241,231 total input tokens). This is
  the **total-spend story**: it is dominated by the two expensive rename tasks, where tier-2 is
  at or below parity.
- **Median of per-task A2/A0 ratios = 1.56.** This is the **typical-small-task story**: a
  representative cheap/callers session still costs ~1.6× baseline because of the fixed
  per-session overhead (see below).
- Control comparison: **ΣA1/ΣA0 = 1.483**; **ΣA2/ΣA1 = 0.781** — tier-2 cut with-skill spend
  ~22% against the same-batch tier-1 control.

Neither number substitutes for the other: pooled 1.16 does not mean a typical task costs 16%
extra, and median 1.56 does not mean total spend rose 56%.

## Correctness

- **Deterministic-grader pool** (`rr-self-discoverfiles-001` + `ci-self-openhealthy-callers-001`,
  hand-verified keys): A2 mean **1.00** (6/6 reps) vs A0 mean **0.71** (A1 mean 0.83 — one
  discoverfiles rep failed under tier-1).
- **Callers regime** (median): runpipeline 0.07 → **0.80** (+0.73), openhealthy 0.43 → **1.00**
  (+0.57) — identical lifts in A1 and A2; the cost gate did not talk codex out of the index
  where it wins.
- **Cheap + rename regimes**: correctness medians unchanged vs A0 (cheap 1.00 → 1.00; renames
  0.00 → 0.00 and 1.00 → 1.00).

## Per-regime findings

**Renames (rr-\*):** the grep-centric rename chain worked as designed — A2 median `mcp_calls`
dropped to **0** on both tasks (from 12 and 8 under tier-1), total tool calls fell (20 → 13,
25 → 20), and token cost went **below no-skill parity** (0.97, 0.83; tier-1 was 1.79, 1.06).
Correctness held (discoverfiles 3/3 under A2 vs 2/3 under A1 on the deterministic grader).
This regime is where the entire pooled improvement comes from.

**Callers (ci-\*):** the index is kept where it wins: +0.73 and +0.57 median correctness over
baseline at ~2.1–2.5× tokens. The cost is the price of 2 MCP round-trips in a codex session;
A1 and A2 are indistinguishable here in both tokens and correctness.

**Cheap (cf-\*):** a structural **fixed session tax** of ~1.56×, identical to the tier-1
control (A1/A0 and A2/A0 agree within ~1% on all four cheap+callers tasks). The mechanism
evidence: on `cf-self-index-hooks-001` and `ci-self-openhealthy-callers-001` the per-rep call
profiles are *exactly equal* across A1 and A2 (1 MCP/1 tool and 2 MCP/2 tool respectively),
yet the with-skill arms cost ~32k–71k tokens more than A0 — and on the other two tasks A2 made
*fewer* median calls than A1 (3 → 2) with no token reduction. The overhead is therefore the
per-step re-billing of the 11 MCP tool schemas + AGENTS.md, not extra calls. **Content edits
cannot fix this**; tier-3 schema-slimming (fewer/leaner exposed tools) is the lever.
